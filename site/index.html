First completed daily draw

After the UTC day ended, OpenVRF selected ticket #1. Its owner received the full 0.000625 test ETH prize. The request and the fulfilled callback are separate transactions. Today's live daily pool above can be empty even though yesterday's draw was paid.

Randomness request · Winning ticket and prize payment

Hourly trader payout

The hook processed the swap's hour, then paid 0.000875 test ETH to the trader: 0.0004375 cashback and 0.0004375 as the top trader. The setup transaction also executed the reserved buyback.

Staking: a second way to earn tickets

The test wallet staked 1,000,000 IRL and claimed ticket #2 through the hook. It also claimed 0.000375 test ETH in yield that the earlier swap had sent to the staking contract. Claiming the ticket immediately applied the contract's 24-hour unstake lock to this test deposit.

How the pool launched

All one billion IRL were minted directly to the vault for the launch allocation. It owns one permanent position (80%) and ten conditional positions (2% each). The tokens are held by Uniswap v4 PoolManager.
